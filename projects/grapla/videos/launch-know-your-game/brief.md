# Launch: Know the game. Know your game.

## Objective
Twenty seconds that say what Grapla is, show the app, and state the price model. The PRD §18 proving production: SVG title, p5 background, Three.js product shot, FFmpeg assembly, no timeline engine yet.

## Audience and placement
BJJ practitioners on Instagram, TikTok and the App Store preview slot. Portrait 1080x1920, 30 fps, 20 s, silent first cut.

## Content (from GraplaWeb/docs/brand.md; numbers are a 2026-10 snapshot)
1. 0 to 4 s: wordmark, then "Know the game. Know your game."
2. 4 to 15 s: the phone. BASE dashboard, ATLAS position page, SESSION detail. One label each: "BASE · your BJJ at a glance", "ATLAS · 600+ entities, 4,000+ transitions", "TRAINING LOG · voice or text, no account".
3. 15 to 20 s: "600+ entities. 4,000+ transitions. Zero guesswork." then "Free on the App Store. One-time Pro unlock." then the wordmark with "Yours forever. No renewal. No server. Just BJJ."

## Constraints
Brand voice rules in `brand/brand.json`: no exclamation marks, numbers over adjectives, CAPS for UI nouns, accent never a large fill, zero radius, JetBrains Mono only. Screenshots and wordmark are owned; font is OFL.
