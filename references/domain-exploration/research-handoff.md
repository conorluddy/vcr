# Shared research records and worked handoff

These are illustrative workspace notes, not a schema or a new approval flow. Use one compact note for a quick pass; expand only the records relevant to an unresolved decision. No production subject or assets belong in the harness.

## One record per responsibility

| Record | Owner | Retain once | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source S | Domain | Creator, title, original locator, source/access dates, read extent, claim-specific verification basis, origin/independence, refresh trigger | All research pillars cite S |
| Observation O | Visual Research | S plus exact page/region/time span, what was inspected, observed features, inference limits | Story, Art, Image |
| Finding F | Domain | Exact claim, S/O links, support/contrary evidence, scope, uncertainty | Story claim registry; Data when quantitative |
| Reading C | Culture | O/F links, audience scope, intended/reported/inferred meanings, competing reading, freshness trigger | Brief, Story, Image, Adaptation |
| Selection I | Image | Job, candidates, placement/motion inspection, selection reason, gaps | Story, Art, execution craft |
| Asset A | Shared asset concern | Original, acquisition, rights evidence or unknown, attribution, permitted use, derivatives | Sound, Light, Materials and Image refer to A |

A source consulted for ideas is not automatically a licensed asset. A signed provenance record is not proof of a depicted claim. Preserve originals; record derivative links once. Metadata presence can be checked structurally; support, independence, permissions and creative relevance need evaluation.

## Evidence and conflicting defaults

Every ledger's historical “confirmed” label is a reported research result, not a new verification by VCR. Abstract-only, snippet-only and secondary-only support remains partial. For a consequential claim, read the actual supporting passage and retain the precise claim, locator, scope and read extent; otherwise mark unresolved and omit it from factual copy or choose a treatment that does not depend on it. Current legal requirements, platform limits and renderer behaviour must be checked at time of use against the relevant primary source and version. No inherited research date guarantees currency.

Uncalibrated stroke widths, colour tolerances and alignment proposals are advisory diagnostics. The brief may select a value with its units, viewing/encoding conditions and rationale; absence of a value is not permission to choose whichever historical proposal is strictest. Art owns palette/stroke judgement; Sound owns the shared beat-alignment tolerance consumed by Motion and Pacing. Until calibrated, record actual frame offset and review it in motion rather than imposing a universal pass threshold.

Plan narration budget uses words divided by the whole beat slot, including room for pauses. Rendered speech-rate diagnostics may exclude marked pauses and must say so. A plan budget pass does not establish intelligibility, reading access or rendered rate. English study defaults must not silently become locale-independent gates.

## Quick pass: one decision note

All bracketed content below is a placeholder; no empirical claim or rendered result is asserted.

- Brief question Q: which view makes [RELATION] understandable at [PLACEMENT]?
- Source S1: [original locator], [date], [passage actually read], scope [BOUNDARY].
- Observation O1: at [region/span], [ELEMENT-A] changes relative to [ELEMENT-B]. Unseen construction settings remain unknown.
- Finding F1: [limited relation supported by S1/O1]. Broader [CLAIM] remains unverified and is excluded from copy.
- Reference principle: preserve the visible relation; do not copy the reference's surface treatment.
- Audience hypothesis C1: [AUDIENCE] may recognise [CUE]. No audience check exists; recognition remains assumed.
- Image candidates: I1 has stronger atmosphere but hides [RELATION]; reject for the explanation slot. I2 exposes the relation at placement; select provisionally. Keep I1 only as a reference, with no reuse permission inferred.
- Revised decision: remove reliance on [CUE] so the core message survives without recognition. Record this in the brief; retain C1 as unresolved context.
- Story handoff: establish the relation, show one change, then hold its result. Use owning reading/motion rules when timing the beats.
- Render review packet: [actual render ID], [evidence frames/span], [encoded-file inspection], [observed issue]. These fields stay empty until a real render exists. File only findings supported by that render; no invented approval or successful test.

## Deeper pass

Expand Q into alternative explanations; trace independent source origins; inspect the full temporal span; compare formal references; investigate consequential audience readings; test image candidates across crops and full motion; update the selected brief decision. Stop when the decision is adequately supported, the declared budget is reached, or evidence cannot support the proposed claim. Hand off an unresolved decision with its consequence instead of manufacturing certainty.

Validation of this abstract example checks the handoff structure only. Threshold calibration and audience effectiveness require real workspace evidence and human review; they are not prerequisites for using these advisory references.
