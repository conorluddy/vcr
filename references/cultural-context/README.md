## Integration notes and open decisions

- **Audience meaning remains a hypothesis.** Recognition, interpretation, emotion and engagement are different questions. Model inference is not community testimony. Judged findings remain advisory under `REVIEW-*`; the report cannot certify cultural meaning.
- **Evidence limits.** The supplied ledger includes abstract-only confirmation, secondary accounts and sources unverified at primary. Some underlying studies concern advertising or isolated cues; transfer to complete creative pieces needs judgement. Import does not independently reverify these findings.
- **Ownership.** [Domain exploration](../domain-exploration/README.md) owns generic source evaluation; [visual research](../visual-research/README.md) owns formal reference analysis and transfer. This reference owns audience-scoped meanings, recognition assumptions and contextual freshness. Story develops concepts; Art Direction selects treatment; Adaptation handles variants; the brief records decisions. [Image direction](../image-direction/README.md) owns image briefs, asset selection and set coherence.
- **Before distillation.** Reconcile duplicate source, date, locator and storage criteria with Domain. Reference-specific recheck triggers belong here; generic source freshness belongs to Domain. All candidate rules remain heuristic, with no automatic taste gates.
- **Boundary.** Specific cultural references and current trends belong in the production workspace and need research at time of use. Templates are illustrative, not implemented schemas. Permissions remain a shared provenance concern, with no new approval mechanism.
