## Integration notes and open decisions

- **Advisory selection criteria.** The report's selection gates were normalised to proposed criteria. No automatic delivery or approval gate is implemented. Missing or incompatible terms remain explicit unresolved use decisions in the existing brief and review process.
- **Source standards versus workflow proposals.** All candidate rule tiers are `heuristic`: adopting metadata terms, derivative models and reproducibility guidance as VCR requirements is a proposed transfer. Actual standards and licence obligations retain their own scope. These rules do not certify compliance or dictate a shipped manifest schema.
- **Evidence limits.** Import does not independently reverify the supplied ledger. Some learning effects are secondary-only or abstract-only; instructional findings do not establish that decorative imagery is inappropriate for expressive work. Journalism norms apply to records and evidence, not all disclosed invention.
- **Metadata is not proof.** It records asserted history and may be missing or wrong. Neither plausible appearance nor credentials establish depicted truth. A selected generated file is the asset of record; input notes do not promise identical regeneration.
- **Ownership.** [Domain exploration](../domain-exploration/README.md) supplies subject knowledge; [visual research](../visual-research/README.md) supplies reference analysis; [cultural context](../cultural-context/README.md) supplies audience meanings. Image Direction owns image purpose, medium choice, creation briefs, selection and set coherence. Story owns the concept, Art Direction owns composition and treatment, and FX owns preparation techniques. Other pillars retain their thresholds.
- **Before distillation.** Deduplicate provenance, storage, source-type and context-verification candidates with the sibling references and shared asset-manifest concern. Reference status alone does not authorise placement, but a reference may become a deliverable after acquisition and suitability are assessed.
- **Boundary.** Templates and manifest fields are illustrative proposals. Production briefs, assets and records stay in the selected workspace; generated previews and temporary derivatives belong in ignored output directories. Judged findings remain advisory under `REVIEW-*`.

For lens/viewpoint, focus, shot scale and continuity decisions, read [camera direction](camera-direction.md).
