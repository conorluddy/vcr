# Sound design

Advisory and uncalibrated.

Consult each applicable rule in the referenced section before applying its limits; never use thresholds from memory. Read one section at a time and preserve provisional status.

Read the [sound routes](../../references/sound-design/README.md), then the sections needed. Establish placement, sound-on/off use, audio's role, script, target, available stems and provenance. Scale the work to the request and reuse settled sonic direction. Decide what each voice, music, effect or silence contributes and which events need emphasis; make room for narration and preserve important information in muted playback (`PACE-SOUND-01`). Shorten copy or revise timing before forcing speech into a fixed slot (`SOUND-NARRATION-WPM`).

Use §3–5 for cue and music decisions: choose music whose structure leaves space for speech and edit at phrases or loop boundaries; associate each meaningful cue with its visual event and intended onset, keep source/licence information with the workspace's existing records, and plan loop transitions where relevant (`SOUND-SYNC-LEAD`, `SOUND-SYNC-LAG`, `SOUND-LOOP-SEAM`). Use §2 and §7–9 for voice intelligibility, dynamics and pauses. Produce audio separately from frame capture (`SOUND-NO-LIVE-CAPTURE`); capture does not synchronize page audio. The cue-list format is not yet defined.

When speech is hard to follow, compare voice and bed in the affected passage and check playback on the intended device before changing the mix; revise the masking source or timing supported by that evidence. Verify decoded output and, where available, separate stems for loudness, peak, balance and sync; identify measurement method, evidence and gaps (`SOUND-LOUDNESS-INTEGRATED`, `SOUND-TRUE-PEAK`). No VCR sound check exists; without stems, balance is uncertain. Human listening is needed for perceptual quality. Hand caption access to accessibility, narration-versus-picture timing to pacing, and final file requirements to delivery. Record cue decisions and unresolved sync, licensing or measurement questions in the workspace.
