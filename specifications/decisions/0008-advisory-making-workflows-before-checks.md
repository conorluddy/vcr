# 0008. Advisory making workflows before checks

Date: 2026-11-08
Status: accepted

## Context

The pillar extraction audit and focused task trials found reusable creation procedures beyond typography. ADR 0006 sequences skills after data and checks; ADR 0007 permits early advisory agents only. The owner explicitly authorised publication of the reviewed workflows.

## Options considered

1. Wait for every pillar's data and checks, leaving making guidance inside large references.
2. Publish advisory making workflows now, retaining source lookup, evidence limits and independent review.

## Decision

Option 2. Extend ADR 0007's early-publication exception to making workflows. Skills cite current rule IDs, look up limits rather than duplicate thresholds, load selected sections, preserve tool-independent craft and workspace boundaries, and distinguish manual judgement from implemented measurements. Advisory workflows and reviewers cannot approve work or create new gates. Publication does not imply calibration, safety certification or completion of review pages. The reviewed lighting and materials workflows ship separately. The existing CLI-only review workflow is an early subset of plugin P4.

## Consequences

Creation guidance becomes discoverable across the bounded pillar set. Rule renaming must update workflow citations as well as agents. Maintain focused routing and artifact evidence; retain incomplete measurements explicitly. Data, checks and reviewer calibration remain separate work under ADR 0006. Draft evaluation records remain historical snapshots; live skills are canonical instructions.

## Revisit when

A workflow invents an unavailable check, duplicated thresholds drift, or advisory outputs are treated as approval or calibrated gates.
