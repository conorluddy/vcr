# Request

Develop a visual direction for a short series that explains a process in three steps. The brief asks for a calm, precise feel and a clear progression from overview to detail. Each frame has a short heading and one simple diagram; the first diagram is the focal point. Output will be landscape. No palette, typeface, exact dimensions, audience research, series tokens or final delivery file have been supplied.

Recommend a direction that a designer can use for an overview and a detail frame. Describe any alternatives you consider and the reason for your choice. Work from the supplied information and identify anything that still needs a decision or inspection.
