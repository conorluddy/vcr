# Routing expectations — evaluator only

1–2: primary art direction; creation and local hierarchy revision respectively.
3–4: neighbouring workflows. Motion owns choreography; image direction owns imagery selection. Art direction supplies focal intent or visual language as appropriate, without taking over execution.
5–6: unrelated as primary tasks: delivery owns encoded-file QC; story owns script and narrative order. A visual handoff may become relevant later, but does not make either request an art-direction task.
