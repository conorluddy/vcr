## Interpretation

Finished written guidance and representative synthetic outputs do not establish production performance. Missing audio, source geometry, destination requirements, human listening and full temporal evidence remain explicitly unassessed where applicable. Browser capture was blocked by the local sandbox in the supplemental narrative trial; source validation is not rendered inspection. Accessibility review is advisory, with no safety or conformance certification. Human approval remains separate.

No new CLI API, schema, rule threshold, calibrated gate or installed skill was introduced. Follow-ups in the individual case directories distinguish remaining reference research questions from the targeted source contradictions corrected during this pass.
