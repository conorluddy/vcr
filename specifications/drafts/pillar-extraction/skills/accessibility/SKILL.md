# Accessibility

Unpublished draft: links assume relocation to `skills/accessibility/SKILL.md`. Advisory and uncalibrated; this is not a conformance or safety certification.

Consult each applicable rule in the referenced section before applying its limits; never use thresholds from memory. Read one section at a time and preserve provisional status.

Read the [accessibility routes](../../references/accessibility/README.md), then only the relevant section. Determine audience, placements, language, sound-on/off context, required variants and any stated standard or jurisdiction. Scale the work to the request and reuse settled access decisions. Map each essential fact and meaningful sound to its visual, spoken or text equivalent; identify gaps before choosing variants. Record the required decisions in the existing brief or workspace notes.

- For captions, use §3: plan accurate speech, meaningful non-speech, speakers, timing and placement (`A11Y-CAPTION-SYNC`, `A11Y-CAPTION-PLACEMENT`); typography owns caption setting and safe areas (`TYPO-CAPTION-*`, `TYPO-SAFE`).
- For visual information, use §4 and §9: decide whether integrated or separate description and still-image alt text are needed; preserve essential relationships (`A11Y-AD-COVERAGE`).
- For colour and graphics, use §5–6: add labels or shape where colour alone carries meaning (`A11Y-COLOR-ALONE`); inspect meaningful graphic contrast and distinguishability (`A11Y-CONTRAST-GRAPHIC`, `A11Y-CVD-DISTINCT`). Text contrast belongs to `TYPO-CONTRAST`.
- For motion and flashing, use §1–2: design a faithful reduced-motion variant where required (`A11Y-MOTION-REDUCED-VARIANT`), and identify possible flash or pattern concerns (`A11Y-FLASH-GENERAL`, `A11Y-FLASH-RED`, `A11Y-PATTERN-STRIPES`). Do not infer safety from stills or sampled frames.

Choose burned-in or sidecar captions for each placement after considering viewer control, player support and possible duplication; time them to the actual version. Where narration cannot describe an essential visual, plan an integrated rewrite or a separate description and check its timing against available pauses. For a reduced-motion variant, preserve information and hierarchy while replacing the triggering movement.

Inspect the actual deliverables and sidecars at intended viewing conditions; seek human playback and caption/description review. No VCR accessibility analyser exists. Flash analysis requires validated full-frame measurement of decoded delivery, including loop seams; absent that evidence, report unable to assess, never safe. Record what was inspected, missing measurements, variant decisions and the owning rule IDs; hand timing to pacing, text setting to typography, and file/sidecar integrity to delivery. Human review and approval remain necessary.
