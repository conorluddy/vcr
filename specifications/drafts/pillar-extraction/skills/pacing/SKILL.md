# Pacing

Draft, advisory and uncalibrated. Links are deployment-relative: they target the final `skills/pacing/SKILL.md` location.

Look up applicable rule definitions in the referenced section or canonical rules data before applying a limit; never use thresholds from memory. Read one section at a time and preserve provisional status.

Start with the brief's duration and audio mode, selected content, existing `beats.json` and markers, and any timing or render evidence. Preserve the story's intended progression and mandatories. If the material cannot fit, identify the tradeoff and propose a content change rather than silently removing meaning.

Scale the work to the request: revise the affected span for a local note; allocate the full sequence only when its structure or duration needs it. Allocate time to each beat by its purpose and cognitive load. Give key information a readable settled hold, allow the payoff to register, and use variation and rests deliberately. Compare narration and visual timing, check the sound-off story where relevant, and adjust cuts against markers when they serve the edit. Update the existing beat plan when timing changes, rather than maintaining a competing schedule.

For feedback such as “rushed” or “drags”, locate the affected span and compare the settled reading time, narration load, pauses and changes in information with the intended beat. Adjust the supported cause, then compare that span in context; a slow move and a long editorial hold have different owners.

Read only the needed route in [pacing](../../references/pacing-editor/README.md): §3–5 for rhythm and load, §9 for sound-off, §11–12 for measurement and critique, and §15 and §20 for creation and checks. `PACE-BEAT-*` owns beat and cut rhythm, `PACE-NARR-03` and `PACE-NARR-04` the visual relationship to narration and `PACE-SOUND-01` the mute test; `TYPO-HOLD` and `TYPO-CAPTION-*` own text and caption timing, `SOUND-NARRATION-WPM` narration rate, and `MOTION-CHOR-003` movement within a beat.

Compare beat boundaries, holds and total duration with the brief and inspect the result at known fps. Plan checks cover only a subset; there is no pacing render check, and word-level narration timings may be unavailable. Report manual observations, missing timing evidence and uncalibrated heuristics without presenting them as gates. Record revised durations and holds in the existing beat plan, regenerate markers when needed, and hand affected ranges and unresolved tradeoffs to story, motion, typography and sound.
