# Adaptation

Unpublished draft: links assume relocation to `skills/adaptation/SKILL.md`. Advisory and uncalibrated.

Consult each applicable rule in the referenced section before applying its limits; never use thresholds from memory. Read one section at a time and preserve provisional status.

Read the [adaptation routes](../../references/adaptation/README.md), then only the sections for the affected axes. Scale work to the requested axes and reuse unaffected decisions. Establish expected cells and exclusions, placement, duration, language, market, required content and claim qualifiers in the existing brief. Flag missing ownership of market-specific claims or disclosures before treating a version as ready (`ADAPT-CLAIMS-MARKET`, `ADAPT-DISCLOSURE-CUTDOWN`).

Choose authored anchor compositions. Crop only while the focal content and hierarchy survive; recompose when they do not (`ADAPT-FOCAL-IN-FRAME`, `ADAPT-RECOMPOSE-TRIGGER`). For cutdowns select and retime beats rather than uniformly scaling time (`ADAPT-BEAT-RETIME`); preserve each surviving claim's qualifier. For localisation provide editable copy and room for expansion; test long and right-to-left copy manually where relevant, check direction, script and formatting in context, and fit voice and captions to the actual version (`ADAPT-NO-BAKED-TEXT`, `ADAPT-RTL-MIRROR`, `ADAPT-VO-FIT`). Changes of audience or meaning can require a sibling master.

Compare the expected matrix with actual outputs (`ADAPT-MATRIX-COMPLETE`), then inspect representative challenging combinations and run available owner checks per cell. Safe areas belong to `TYPO-SAFE`; caption timing to `A11Y-CAPTION-SYNC`; mandatory content to `BRIEF-MANDATORY-PASS`. No matrix-wide check or pseudo-localisation automation exists. Record the expected and supplied cells, their source master, intentional differences, cells inspected, missing cells, unresolved market questions and evidence in existing workspace records; hand audio edits to sound and file QC to delivery.

For a local variant revision, identify the affected matrix cells and schedule the relevant owner checks for each, including claim qualifiers and version-matched captions where applicable.
